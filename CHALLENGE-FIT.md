# Kaleva Local: fit to Challenge 2

Based on the sponsor-provided Junction_Kaleva_Media.pdf, inspected in full on 24 October 2026. This is project context, not an instruction to implement its separate sports challenge.

## Chosen community and value

Start with international workers, their families and students in Oulu. Keep the groups distinct in reader context and pilot evaluation; do not assume equal needs or willingness to pay. English is the first working reader language and Finnish connects the community’s questions to local readers. Other languages need competent reviewers before they are offered.

The product’s unit of value is a question that receives a source-based, understandable answer. An RSS headline is a reporting lead, not a ready explainer. The mobile web format lets participants open a link without installing an app. Messaging, email, audio and other delivery channels should be chosen with the first pilot group rather than invented here.

## Editorial operation

- Gathering: real Kaleva RSS and an Oulu open-data sample; dates and source failures are visible. The historical library result demonstrates why retrieval is not verification.
- Selection: community questions, unverified observations and source leads enter a local queue. The editor states a reason and chooses Information, Understanding, Usefulness, Emotion, Break or Community.
- Reporting: the deterministic prepared demonstration uses the inspected 2024 survey. New RSS leads require original reporting and cannot be published through the unrelated fixture.
- Copy desk: bilingual factual and language sign-offs, a seeded unsupported-claim gate, exact reviewed revision, invalidated approval on edits, visible corrections.
- Accountability: private community contributions, source access, explicit AI disclosure and fictional reviewer identity. Public demo controls are not production authentication.

No working AI model is connected. Automated gathering works; drafting and translation are labelled simulations. A production trial needs qualified editors and language reviewers, especially for high-stakes topics and less-supported languages.

## Community and commercial validation

Proposed, not already completed: invite each reader group to listening sessions, choose useful questions with participants and ask them to evaluate the explanations. No trusted-voice partnership or customer is claimed.

Primary experiment: €900 for six weeks, up to 100 employees/family members, 12 explainers and six digests. The employer buys access and cannot suppress stories, choose favourable coverage or inspect individual reading. Additional hypotheses are €5.90 monthly Local Plus and €300 monthly clearly separated advertising.

Measure repeat use, trust, correction frequency, actual paid acceptance and workload by activity: reporting, sources, English review, Finnish review, corrections, support and hosting. Total costs must be known before asserting a margin on €900. The demo claims no cost saving, break-even result or validated demand. A simulated checkout is not revenue or willingness to pay.
